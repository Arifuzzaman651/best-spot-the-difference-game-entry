Spot the Difference — Three Worlds: a complete spot-the-difference game in plain canvas 2D. Two near-identical pictures side by side, click a difference in either panel to ring it on both, with a counter of how many remain, a count-up timer, hints, and three hand-drawn worlds of rising difficulty — Garden Morning (7 differences), Riverside Market (8), Space Station (10). Every scene is drawn procedurally from one recipe, so the differences are hidden by design: a sun that lost its smile, a balloon that changed colour, a clock showing a different time, a tether that vanished in orbit.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open game/index.html directly. Click a difference to mark it, H for a hint, R to restart the level, 1 / 2 / 3 to switch worlds. `?demo=N&level=L` pre-marks the first N differences of level L.
